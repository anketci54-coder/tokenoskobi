\# TOKENOSKOBI ENGINEERING MANIFEST



\## Vision



Tokenoskobi is not a trading bot.



Tokenoskobi is a Decision Operating System.



The objective is to build a deterministic, testable, auditable and maintainable engineering platform.



\---



\## Core Principles



\- Fail Closed by Default

\- Evidence First

\- Human Approval Before Critical Actions

\- Immutable Ledger

\- Platform Independence

\- Deterministic Runtime

\- Health Check Before Release



\---



\## Development Workflow



Design

↓

Implementation

↓

Smoke Test

↓

Commit



No feature is complete until it passes the engineering health check.
